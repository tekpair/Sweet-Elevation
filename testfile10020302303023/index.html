Handling, shipping & cancellations

When your box arrives: Refrigerate immediately. Boxes ship uninsulated, so timing matters. Treats stay fresh 3–5 days refrigerated, longer if frozen.

Shipping time: Most boxes arrive within 3–5 business days via UPS Ground Saver or USPS Priority Mail (depending on destination). Boxes ship Monday–Wednesday so they don't sit in a warehouse over the weekend.

Need to cancel or report an issue? Email sweetelevationbakery@gmail.com. Subscriptions can be paused or canceled anytime before the next month's box is packed. Once a box is packed and labeled for shipment, that order can no longer be canceled — but the subscription itself can still be stopped before the next cycle.

Damaged shipments: If your box arrives significantly damaged, email us within 24 hours of delivery with photos and we'll make it right.

All sales final. Due to the perishable nature of our products, we don't accept returns or exchanges outside of damaged shipments.

Ingredients & Allergens

Full ingredient lists for each item are included on the label card shipped with your box. Ingredients vary by month based on the featured lineup.

Allergen notice: All items are made and packaged in a facility that processes wheat, gluten, and tree nuts. We cannot guarantee against cross-contamination. If you have a severe allergy, please do not order.
