Elevation Jars Launch Giveaway Official Rules

No purchase necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning.

These are the official rules for the Elevation Jars Launch Giveaway (the "Giveaway"). No purchase necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning. By entering, you agree to these Official Rules.

1. Sponsor

This giveaway is sponsored by Sweet Elevation Bakery.

2. Giveaway Period

The Elevation Jars Launch Giveaway begins on Monday, July 27, 2026 at 12:00 PM Eastern Time and ends on Sunday, August 2, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time.

3. Eligibility

Open only to legal residents of the 48 contiguous United States and Washington, D.C., age 18 or older at the time of entry. Void where prohibited by law.

Residents of Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, U.S. territories, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands, and individuals using APO/FPO/DPO military or diplomatic addresses or P.O. Boxes for prize delivery are not eligible.

Employees, owners, and immediate family or household members of Sweet Elevation Bakery are not eligible to win.

4. How to Enter

During the giveaway period, eligible participants may receive one automatic entry by purchasing Elevation Jars from Sweet Elevation Bakery.

No purchase is necessary to enter or win. To enter without making a purchase, complete the official free entry form.

Enter for Free

Limit one entry per person, regardless of entry method. Duplicate entries may be disqualified. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning.

5. Prizes

Three winners will be selected.

First Place

One Elevation Jar Care Package with a total package value of up to $150. The care package will be curated and selected by Sweet Elevation Bakery at its sole discretion. Flavors, jar varieties, and included items will be chosen by Sweet Elevation Bakery and are subject to availability, production schedule, and shipping suitability. The stated value includes the total curated package, including edible items, jars, packaging, presentation materials, and related preparation/handling. It does not represent $150 of edible product alone.

Second Place

One $75 Sweet Elevation gift card or online store code. Winner may choose either in-store use or Shopify online store use.

Third Place

One $25 Sweet Elevation gift card or online store code. Winner may choose either in-store use or Shopify online store use.

Total Approximate Retail Value: Up to $250.

Prizes have no cash value and may not be exchanged, transferred, or substituted by the winner.

6. Winner Selection

Winners will be selected at random from all eligible entries received during the giveaway period. Winners will be selected on or about Monday, August 3, 2026.

Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

7. Winner Notification

Winners will be contacted by Sweet Elevation Bakery by email, phone, or social media message. Winners must respond within 48 hours of notification.

If a winner does not respond within 48 hours, is ineligible, or cannot accept the prize, Sweet Elevation Bakery may select an alternate winner.

8. Shipping / Pickup

Shipping is included for the first place prize if Sweet Elevation Bakery determines the selected items are suitable for shipping.

Winner must provide a valid physical street address within the 48 contiguous United States or Washington, D.C. Sweet Elevation Bakery does not ship giveaway prizes to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, U.S. territories, APO/FPO/DPO military or diplomatic addresses, or P.O. Boxes.

Sweet Elevation Bakery is not responsible for delays, incorrect addresses, damaged packages caused by carrier handling, weather delays, or circumstances outside of its control once shipped.

Certain prizes may be available for local pickup or online redemption only.

9. Food Allergy Notice

Products are made in a bakery that handles common allergens, including wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, and other allergens. Sweet Elevation Bakery is not an allergen-free facility.

10. Publicity

By accepting a prize, winners allow Sweet Elevation Bakery to announce their first name, last initial, city/state, and/or social media handle for winner announcement purposes, unless prohibited by law.

11. Platform Disclaimer

This giveaway is sponsored by Sweet Elevation Bakery and is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook, Instagram, or Meta. By entering, participants release Facebook, Instagram, and Meta from responsibility for this promotion.

12. Sponsor’s Rights

Sweet Elevation Bakery reserves the right to cancel, modify, or suspend the giveaway if fraud, technical issues, shipping limitations, inventory limitations, or other circumstances affect the fairness or proper administration of the giveaway.
