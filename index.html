our story

From the ADKs to the Capital District.

Jennifer grew up in Wilmington, NY — a little Adirondack town at the foot of Whiteface Mountain. Years ago she ran a bakery up there called Cake Placid. Life got busy, the bakery closed, and the recipes went into the drawer.

Sweet Elevation is what happens when those recipes come back out — with all the warmth of the mountains, settled into a storefront in Latham.
