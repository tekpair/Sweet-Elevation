Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Sweet Elevation Bakery is a small, family-run bakery in Latham, NY. We don't run our own ad networks, we don't sell customer data, and we don't have a marketing machine collecting information on you. But we do use third-party services (like Square, Shopify, Google, and Formspree) to run the business — and those services collect some data when you interact with our site. This policy explains what.

1. Who we are

Sweet Elevation Bakery is a sole proprietorship operated by Jennifer Gates as a registered DBA in New York State. Our location is 356 Troy Schenectady Rd, Latham, NY 12110. The website at sweetelevationbakery.com is operated by Jennifer Gates DBA Sweet Elevation Bakery. If you have questions about this policy, email us at sweetelevationbakery@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Our website doesn't have user accounts, doesn't store passwords, and doesn't ask you to log in. That said, third-party services that power parts of our site do collect data when you visit or transact.

Data you give us directly

  • Custom quote forms — when you submit a custom order request, we collect your name, email, phone, event details, and anything else you write in the form. This is processed by Formspree and emailed to us.
  • Careers applications — name, email, phone, work history, references, and anything else you submit. Also processed by Formspree.
  • Wedding & event inquiries — same as above: name, email, phone, event details.
  • Online orders — when you buy from our online store, your name, shipping address, billing address, and payment info are collected and processed by Square, Shopify, or our payment processor (depending on the product).

Data collected automatically

  • Google Analytics — tracks page visits, time on page, device type, approximate location (city level), and traffic sources. This is anonymous in the sense that we don't see your name — we see aggregate stats like "30 people from Albany visited the store page this week."
  • Google Ads — if you click one of our Google ads, Google tracks the click and may set a cookie to measure whether you eventually converted (e.g., submitted a quote or placed an order).
  • Cookies — small pieces of data stored in your browser by Google Analytics, Google Ads, Shopify, and similar services. They help these tools function and measure traffic. You can clear or block them in your browser settings at any time.

3. Who else handles your data

We rely on third-party services to run the business. When you interact with our site, some of your data passes through them:

  • Square — processes payments and (when applicable) ships orders. Square has its own privacy policy at squareup.com/legal/privacy.
  • Shopify — when we use Shopify Buy Buttons for the Bake Club Box and online store, Shopify handles checkout, shipping calculation, and order management. Shopify's privacy policy is at shopify.com/legal/privacy.
  • Formspree — receives your form submissions (custom quotes, careers, wedding inquiries) and emails them to us. Their policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google Analytics & Google Ads — usage tracking and ad measurement. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • DoorDash — if you click through to order delivery, DoorDash handles your order under their privacy policy.
  • Facebook & Instagram — if you click our social links and engage with our pages there, Meta's data practices apply.
  • Pirate Ship & carriers (USPS, UPS) — your shipping address is shared with these services to ship your order.

4. How we use your data

The data we collect is used to:

  • Process your orders and ship products
  • Respond to custom quote and event inquiries
  • Review job applications
  • Understand which pages on our site are useful (Google Analytics)
  • Measure whether our ads work (Google Ads conversion tracking)
  • Send you order updates, shipping notifications, and (occasionally) email updates about Bake Club or seasonal offerings — only if you signed up for them

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your information with advertisers beyond what Google Analytics and Google Ads automatically collect as part of their services.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order records are kept indefinitely for tax, accounting, and customer service purposes.
  • Form submissions (quotes, careers, inquiries) are kept until they're no longer relevant, typically 1–3 years.
  • Analytics data is retained per Google's default Google Analytics retention settings (currently 14 months).
  • Cookies expire based on each service's individual cookie policy — usually within a year.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights regarding your data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we have about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix anything that's wrong
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your personal data, except where we're legally required to retain it (e.g., tax records)
  • Opt-out of marketing — unsubscribe from any marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails
  • Opt-out of analytics — install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on to prevent your visits from being tracked

To exercise any of these rights, email sweetelevationbakery@gmail.com. We respond to all reasonable requests within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We don't store payment information ourselves — that's handled by Square, Shopify, and our payment processors, all of which use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and PCI-compliant security. Form data is transmitted over HTTPS.

That said, no online service is 100% secure. We do our best to choose reputable providers, but we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business changes — for example, if we add new services, change payment processors, or update our subscription offerings. When we do, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced on our website and social media.
