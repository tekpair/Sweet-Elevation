Our products are perishable, handmade baked goods that ship uninsulated. We do this because insulated packaging significantly increases cost and our margins are tight. By ordering, you understand:

  • You should refrigerate your box immediately upon arrival.
  • Treats stay fresh 2–3 days refrigerated, or longer if frozen.
  • Hot weather, shipping delays, or extended porch sitting can affect quality. We can't guarantee against this.
  • We don't ship during extreme heat to certain regions when we believe products will arrive damaged.

All sales final. Due to the perishable nature of our products, we don't accept returns or exchanges.
